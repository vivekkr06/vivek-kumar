About

Engineer by training. Product leader by obsession.

I started as a design engineer at ASML — one of the most technically complex environments on earth. That gave me something rare: I'm genuinely comfortable in a room full of engineers debating microservice architecture, and equally comfortable sitting across from a customer trying to understand why their workflow breaks under pressure.

Moving into product felt less like a career change and more like finding the right vantage point. I could finally see the full picture — the user's frustration, the engineer's constraint, the business's obligation — and do something about all three at once.

Today I lead product at ETPA B.V., owning the spot auction trading platform for European energy markets. It's a critical software where downtime creates a P&L event. That context sharpens your instincts fast. I am also leading the core platform stability and reliability initiatives to accomplish 99.95% system uptime.

On a different tangent, I'm currently pursuing an Executive MBA at Amsterdam Business School expanding my ropes on how businesses work. Also, as a side quest I am actively exploring how AI systems can change the way complex B2B workflows operate. Every failure and win is a learning moment and I write about all of it on LinkedIn — the craft, the failures, and the patterns that repeat across industries.

Experience

The roles that shaped how I think

A journey of what I found, what I changed, and what I learned.

Feb 2025 — Present
ETPA B.V.
Energy Trading
Product Lead — Spot auctions & Platform reliability

When I joined ETPA, the challenge wasn't a lack of features — it was a platform built for yesterday's trading volumes trying to operate in today's regulatory environment. I started where I always start: with the traders themselves. Sitting next to them during live auctions, I mapped every friction point that a dashboard would never surface.

That discovery work became the backbone of a roadmap targeting €2M revenue by 2027. But the more important work was infrastructural: I defined SLOs, SLIs, and error budgets for core services — introducing the kind of engineering transparency that lets teams make decisions with confidence rather than gut feel. A decoupled architecture initiative followed, cutting deployment issues by roughly half and putting us on track for 99.95% uptime. In a trading platform, that's not an SLA metric — it's table stakes for enterprise trust.

The platform now runs across 3 squads, meets EU regulatory obligations for ACER and JAO, and has an 85% pilot approval rate from diamond-tier enterprise customers.

↑ 85% enterprise pilot approval ↓ ~50% deployment issues ↓ ~30% MTTD via distributed tracing ACER · JAO compliance SLOs · SLIs · Error budgets

AI Lab

Learning agentic AI — by building with it

I believe the next frontier for B2B product managers is understanding how autonomous agents change workflows. So I'm building one.

Project
Trading Ops Agent

Energy trading operations generate enormous amounts of unstructured signal — auction results, regulatory notifications, counterparty communications, platform alerts. Today, much of this is processed manually by operations teams. I'm building an agentic workflow tool that automates the routing, summarisation, and prioritisation of that signal.

The project is as much a learning exercise as a product: I'm studying how LLMs reason under uncertainty, how to design reliable agent loops, and where human oversight still belongs in the chain. The PM perspective I bring is deliberate — I'm not just building a demo, I'm stress-testing it against real operational pain points I've seen firsthand.

Follow the build →
Signal ingestion
Pulls from auction feeds, regulatory alerts, and platform events — normalises heterogeneous inputs into structured agent context.
Autonomous routing
Agent classifies urgency, assigns ownership, and escalates to humans only when confidence is below threshold — the human-in-the-loop stays intentional.
Ops summarisation
Generates shift-start briefings from overnight event logs — replacing 30-minute manual triage with a structured 2-minute read.
What I'm learning
ReAct prompting · Tool use · Agent evaluation · When to trust the model and when to hard-code the rule.
